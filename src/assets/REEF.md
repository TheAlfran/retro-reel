# Clear-water reef scenery

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Transparent originals are saved as `reef-coral.png` and `reef-seaweed.png` in this folder and packaged by Vite. Both are decorative only, with no fishing interaction. Seaweed pivots at its roots; coral has three varied placements. Distant vegetation, sandbanks, the blue water gradient and animated light shafts are drawn in Phaser to adapt to sidebar and editor sizes.

The game now uses `reef-coral-pixel.png` and `reef-seaweed-pixel.png`: crisp, limited-palette conversions generated from those originals for nearest-neighbor display. The originals remain available as non-destructive source assets.

## reef-coral.png prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent PNG decorative sprite for Retro Reel cozy side-view pixel-art fishing game. One compact coral garden on a low pale sandstone rock: branching salmon-pink coral on left, small lilac fan coral and golden tube sponges on right. Strict side elevation, chunky clean pixel shapes, dark muted colored outlines, simple readable warm palette, softly lit by clear shallow blue water. Entire isolated cluster visible, wide 3:2 silhouette, minimal transparent margins, base level horizontal. No surrounding water, seabed, fish, bubbles, text, frame or scenery. True alpha transparency. Modest detailed game sprite, not photoreal.
